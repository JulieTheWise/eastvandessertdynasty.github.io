About the Chef

Hi! I'm Salila. I was born and live in Vancouver, BC. I have loved baking since I was three years old and I have always wanted to start a baking business, and now I have! I am passionate about finding the right bake for the right person. When I'm not baking (which is not often), I like to roller skate, do musical theatre, babysit, cook, play sports and more. But baking is at the top of the list. I learned to bake from my grammie and mum. My favourite cake that I've baked was my first pride cake. I hope you find the perfect cake for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • Q: How far in advance do I need to order?
    A: Depends on the type and detail but typically two weeks lead time is required.
  • Q: Do you make gluten-free/vegan/allergy-free bakes?
    A: Yes. However, although I thoroughly clean my workspace with every new bake, it is not a nut-free kitchen.
  • Q: Is there minimum/maximum order size?
    A: For cookies, I can make a minimum of 12 and maximum of 100 (with enough notice). For cakes, the minimum is one and the maximum is two larger cakes, or four smaller cakes.
  • Q: What sizes of cake do you make?
    A: The largest single cake I can make is 9 inches diameter or 16 inches square, with multiple layers. For sheet cakes, it is possible to make larger sizes by combining cakes!
