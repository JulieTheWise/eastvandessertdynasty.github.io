About the Chef

Hi! I'm Salila. I was born and live in Vancouver, BC. I have loved baking since I was three years old and I have always wanted to start a baking business, and now I have! I am passionate about finding the right bake for the right person. When I'm not baking (which is not often), I like to roller skate, do musical theatre, babysit, cook, play sports and more. But baking is at the top of the list. I learned to bake from my grammie and mum. My favourite cake that I've baked was my first pride cake. I hope you find the perfect cake for you

Land Acknowledgement
