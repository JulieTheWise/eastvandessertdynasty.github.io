Past Bakes

This is a collection of things that the East Van Dessert Dynasty has baked in the past. You can use this page to get some inspiration, and check out the chef's capabilities.

Watermelon Cake

Price: ~$60Served 12-15 people

This is a watermelon cake that I made for a family special occasion. The cake was red vanilla and on top was fondant.

Chocolate Zucchini Cake With Mocha Buttercream

Price: ~$80Served 15-20 people

This is a chocolate buttercream cake that I made for a family friend as a gift. The cake was a rich, chocolate, zucchini cake, and a creamy mocha buttercream on top.

Inclusive Pride Cake

Price: ~$75Served 18-20 people

As part of my appreciation and connection to the queer community I wanted to make a special tribute cake in hounor of the community and its flag. This cake has sevel layers each of the colours of the rainbow pride flag. The fondant was then done in all the colours of the of the triangular portion of the Inclusive Pride flag as well. The base cakes were vanilla flavoured and the icing was simple vanilla as well. All colouring was done with natural food dies.
